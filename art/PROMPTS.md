# Artwork provenance

Both original PNGs were generated with the **built-in image generation tool**, not the CLI. The generated PNGs are preserved unchanged. The game loads copies from its `images` folder. Sparrow XML supplies pose rectangles and frame offsets. Small menu and health icons are separate programmatically drawn UI artwork in `tools/build_mod.py`.

## `volt-atlas.png`

Use case: stylized-concept. Production game asset: a transparent PNG sprite sheet for an original Friday Night Funkin rhythm-game opponent, named VOLT. Create exactly SIX full-body sprites of the SAME character, in a perfectly regular 3-column by 2-row grid on a 1536x1024 transparent canvas, each cell 512x512. NO borders, NO labels, NO letters, NO shadows outside the character, NO background. All sprites completely contained inside their own cell, with 35px empty margins, feet consistently at y=475 within each cell. Character: charismatic mischievous electric street-DJ robot, chunky deep-purple hoodie, oversized teal sneakers, lime lightning-bolt antenna, dark angular CRT helmet with bright cyan expressive eyes and mouth, short orange bomber vest, cyan microphone with black grille in hand, energetic punk silhouette. Bold thick near-black outlines, flat crisp cel-shaded cartoon shapes, limited purple/cyan/acid yellow palette, highly readable FNF-style 2D game art. Pose layout in row-major order: 1) idle confident crouching bounce stance facing slightly right, microphone near chest; 2) singing LEFT, lean and point left with microphone at mouth; 3) singing DOWN, deep squat helmet lowered and mic close; 4) singing UP, stretch torso and helmet upward, mic up; 5) singing RIGHT, lean right energetically mic forward; 6) celebratory idle variant, lively bounce with free hand raised. Keep face, clothes, colors and body proportions consistent across all six. True transparent alpha around each sprite. No characters besides these six copies. This atlas is directly consumed by a game so exact grid placement and no clipping matter.

The generated poses slightly exceeded the requested grid boundaries. The atlas therefore uses measured rectangles around each complete figure instead of mechanically cutting 512-pixel cells.

## `dynamo.png`

Use case: stylized-concept. Production 2D background for an original Friday Night Funkin mod titled ENERGIZE, stage named The Dynamo. Wide landscape PNG 1536x1024. A neon rooftop pirate-radio arena above a midnight city: chunky black-outlined stylized purple industrial architecture, giant electric cyan reactor ring behind a central DJ platform, huge angular speakers stacked at far left and far right, acid-yellow warning stripes, cables and orange power cells. Dramatic but clean crisp hand-drawn cartoon/cel shading, same visual family as bold underground flash-era rhythm game art. Strong near-black outlines and geometric shapes. Composition designed for gameplay: straight-on side-view camera at performer height, expansive EMPTY flat floor across the lower 28 percent, floor line at y=760, left and right front performance spaces must be unobstructed for characters to be added by engine; background scenery all behind floor line; DJ pedestal at central rear only. Cyan light accents on deep violet and navy scenery, orange accents. Top quarter darker and simpler so colored arrow receptors are visible. Depth from layered distant skyscrapers and storm clouds. No people, no robots, no figures, no text, no words, no letters, no logos, no arrows, no UI. Image extends to every canvas edge, opaque background.
